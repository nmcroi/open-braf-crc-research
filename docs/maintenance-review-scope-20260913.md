## Consequence for the research question

The requested review round is complete within the stated access and extraction limits. Issue 4 remains open: neither this review nor the underlying bounded search identifies regimen-specific outcomes after progression during EC maintenance following oxaliplatin cessation. This is an unresolved evidence search, not proof that a particular strategy works or fails, and not proof that no such data exist anywhere.

The next useful step remains locating appropriately defined aggregate subsequent-treatment outcomes, with the actual prior drugs, unique-patient denominator, treatment starting point and censoring rules. A data-availability enquiry has been drafted separately; this review does not imply that any enquiry was sent.
