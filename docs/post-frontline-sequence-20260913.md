# Subsequent chemotherapy versus continuing EC: evidence and guidance

13 September 2026 · TASK-0004 · ChatGPT/Codex source review; independent review pending.

**Result:** an exact-sequence professional guidance source was located, while direct regimen-specific outcome evidence remains unestablished in this bounded search. A newly screened second-line trial excludes the molecular population. These findings help structure clinical discussion; they do not select treatment for an individual.

## A professional recommendation addressing the exact first-line sequence

The [French TNCD, 13 March 2026](https://www.snfge.org/sites/www.snfge.org/files/tncd/2026-03/tncd_chap-04-cancer-colorectal-metastatique_2026-03-13.pdf), printed p95, explicitly charts second-line FOLFIRI plus anti-VEGF after first-line FOLFOX+EC. Its footnote defines anti-VEGF as bevacizumab or aflibercept. The BRAF/MSS chart on p88 labels that second-line choice expert opinion. Both figures were visually checked. This supports a professional discussion pathway, not comparative efficacy in the exact population or an automatic Dutch treatment recommendation.

The [publisher's current chapter page](https://www.snfge.org/tncd/cancer-colorectal-metastatique) still identifies the March version. Selected relevant sections and figures were reviewed, not all 95 pages. The PDF itself remains at the publisher; no third-party figures are redistributed here.

An apparent advantage between observational groups cannot by itself determine which regimen caused the difference. Absence of an exact randomized comparison also does not mean clinicians have no reasonable next treatment; distinguish professional guidance from demonstrated regimen-specific efficacy.

## Newly screened trial: ULYSSE does not match BRAF V600E

[ULYSSE / NCT07150403](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07150403), PRODIGE115–FFCD2406, EUCT2025-522108-26-00, tests second-line doublet chemotherapy with fruquintinib or bevacizumab. The primary API explicitly excludes BRAF V600E-mutated tumours. The [sponsor synopsis](https://ramdam.ffcd.fr/CUSTOM/GED/20251028/2a7b9987ed.pdf) independently corroborates the requirement for BRAF V600E wild type. Consequently this specific trial is excluded from the BRAF V600E shortlist. This does not rule out all future chemotherapy/fruquintinib research. No participant screening or outreach was attempted.
