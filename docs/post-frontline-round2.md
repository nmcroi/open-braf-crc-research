# Post-frontline outcomes — priority round 2

12 September 2026 · TASK-0004 · bounded primary-source search.

**Result:** no regimen-specific outcome for a cohort explicitly treated after first-line encorafenib/cetuximab plus chemotherapy was established in this search. There is human evidence after prior BRAF inhibition, but it must not be promoted to an exact treatment-sequence match. The useful next filter is prior-treatment-specific activity and eligibility, not overall response in mixed populations.

## What changes in our search?

Mosperafenib remains a **conditional research lead**, not an established next treatment. The small previously exposed subgroup cautions against selecting it from an impressive pooled or BRAF-inhibitor-naive result. Round 3 should check whether relevant European study arms admit prior EC+chemotherapy, whether resistance alterations affect eligibility, and whether recruitment is current. Do not infer that a biomarker test will necessarily find an available treatment.

Separately, established chemotherapy discussions remain relevant to the clinical team. This search does not determine which regimen should be chosen or whether EC should continue beyond progression.

## Access and search limitations

Seventeen English web queries covered exact first-line exposure, BREAKWATER subsequent treatment, EC+nivolumab cohort B, and mosperafenib previously exposed cohorts. Queries are preserved in [search log](../results/post-frontline-round2-search.json). Primary identities were checked through Europe PMC; two article XMLs were retrieved and relevant sections inspected. This was not an exhaustive worldwide search or a full conference-archive screen. No requests sent to investigators. No exact first-line EC+chemotherapy subgroup found is a bounded search result, not evidence that such patients or data do not exist.

A source-identity discrepancy was resolved: PMID41340484 is the **preclinical** mosperafenib paper, not the ctDNA clinical paper (PMID42127914). That correction is separate from the substantive treatment-sequence question.
