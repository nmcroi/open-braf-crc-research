## Stanford Virtual Lab — reachable

https://github.com/zou-group/virtual-lab

An open-source human/AI scientific collaboration framework. Its repository describes nanobody design with experimental testing and links its Nature paper (DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09442-9). It is not a shared BRAF CRC clinical research program.

Practical contribution: independently reproduce a small public-data workflow, document framework failures and submit a focused software issue or pull request following the maintainers' guidance. A BRAF CRC example could be proposed, but adoption or scientific supervision must not be assumed. First prepare a tested example here; ask maintainers before proposing a larger collaboration.
