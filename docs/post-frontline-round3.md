# European access after first-line EC plus chemotherapy — round 3

12 September 2026 · TASK-0004 · Primary registry review by ChatGPT/Codex; independent review pending.

**Useful result:** RO7673396 has a European registration naming the Netherlands Cancer Institute/Antoni van Leeuwenhoek in Amsterdam. This creates a specific eligibility question for acquired RAS resistance, **not a confirmed treatment option**. The public criteria require RAS mutation(s) but do not explicitly establish acceptance of a BRAF V600E tumour that acquired RAS mutations after EC. Several other plausible routes fail current recruitment or treatment-sequence checks.

## What was checked

Seven complete ClinicalTrials.gov API records were downloaded on 12 September; status, eligibility, arm and location sections were read. Three CTIS sections for YO45758 and the ECLYpSe summary were read in the rendered official portal. Twenty-five English web queries covered named candidates and European access. Registry hashes and observations are in [the audit manifest](../results/post-frontline-round3-manifest.json) and [CTIS observations](../results/post-frontline-round3-ctis.json); [search log](../results/post-frontline-round3-search.json). This is a bounded candidate review, not a comprehensive European trial census. Registry status is not site capacity, and absence of an exclusion from a summary is not permission to enroll.

## A biological reason to ask the acquired-RAS question

The sponsor's [AACR 2024 presentation, slide 18](https://www.revmed.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/AACR2024_6236_FINAL-Revised.pdf) describes one BRAF V600E CRC case with acquired KRAS G12V, NRAS G13R and NRAS Q61R after EC. RMC-6236 produced a reported confirmed partial response; listed target diameters fell from 129 to 78 mm (39.5%). Prior sequence was FOLFIRI+bevacizumab, EC, then EC+nivolumab. This is one selected case with limited follow-up, not a response rate, and **not** the exact first-line EC+chemotherapy population. It does support investigating whether acquired RAS can be actionable; it does not show RO7673396 has equivalent activity.

**Falsifiable next question:** does an open European RAS-inhibitor cohort explicitly accept acquired RAS alterations in BRAF V600E CRC after EC+chemotherapy? Rejection of this molecular context in the full protocol, no matching mutation, or no active cohort would remove the enrollment lead. Molecular analysis should be linked to a concrete clinical decision; these records alone do not justify testing, a treatment pause, or a medication change.

## Evaluation after three priority rounds

1. **No established subsequent-regimen efficacy in the exact population was found.** Round 1 separated untreated cohorts and later-line EC; round 2 separated human prior-BRAF activity from the exact sequence. The earlier BREAKWATER supplement describes treatments received, not comparative second-line efficacy.
2. **The access shortlist is sharper.** The original mosperafenib study ended; PF-07799933 CRC expansion excludes prior exposure; ECLYpSe has both a sequence mismatch and an ended-recruitment entry. Rechallenge is a later-sequence question with an inconsistent arm label.
3. **One targeted next step is justified:** primary protocol/cohort clarification for YO45758, especially the NKI/AVL entry, and independent review of these source readings. This replaces repeating broad searches for the same named studies. A subsequent focused round may seek public protocol documents and enrolment criteria; no external outreach has been sent or authorized by this review.

No claim of a new effective treatment, a personal response probability or confirmed eligibility. TASK-0004 remains open; this round is submitted for independent review, not marked clinically validated. CD47 remains parked.

## Access limitations

ISRCTN13713551 HTML redirected to a holding page. The mosperafenib CTIS rendered text was unavailable through web extraction; its indexed primary summary was triangulated with Roche and NIHR. YO45758 and ECLYpSe were instead successfully inspected in a browser. The rendered YO45758 full-information section did not expose detailed cohort-specific eligibility; no full protocol or centre confirmation was obtained. An NCT keyword search for RO7276389/mosperafenib/RG6344 returned zero records; that does not invalidate its ISRCTN/CTIS registration. No restricted patient data or private clinical dossier used.
